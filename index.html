Career Application Toolkit

A practical system for building job-specific CVs and cover letters from verified career evidence.

v0.5.0 · Initial baseline

A better path from evidence to application

The goal is not to sound automated or to force a match. It is to identify the strongest genuine evidence, express it naturally, and deliver a document that reads cleanly for both a hiring team and an applicant-tracking system.

01

Evidence inventory

Capture confirmed work, outcomes, tools, scope, education, and qualifications before writing a single bullet.

02

Role map

Connect the job's real priorities with the closest verified evidence; do not inflate gaps into claims.

03

Natural drafts

Use precise, conversational professional language that makes the applicant's contribution easy to understand.

04

Export QA

Inspect the PDF for factual accuracy, usable text extraction, hierarchy, spacing, and page breaks.

Evidence-first. ATS-aware. Human-readable.
